RANGE - AIM TRAINER

Open index.html in Chrome or Edge. The trainer includes Three.js locally and works offline.

Choose a drill, adjust settings, and press Start training. Move the mouse to aim and click to shoot. For Strafe tracking, hold the left mouse button on the target. Press Escape to pause; click Resume to continue. The session ends when the timer runs out.

Best scores are stored in your browser on this computer. Compare scores using the same session duration and settings. Browser and OS mouse handling can affect sensitivity. This trainer does not simulate CS2 movement, recoil, hitboxes, or game physics.

Three.js r160: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r160/LICENSE
Training references: https://aimlabs.com/articles/aimlabs/train-micros-and-flicking-in-counter-strike-2-with-the-aim-basics-routine/ and https://prosettings.net/blog/how-to-create-perfect-cs2-warmup/

SENSITIVITY: Enter the exact CS2 sensitivity and use the same mouse DPI. The trainer requests unadjusted mouse input to avoid OS acceleration. If your browser cannot supply it, the range falls back to adjusted input and displays that status. Even with raw input, field of view, resolution, and browser behavior can change the feel. Compare a full 360-degree turn on your mousepad.
