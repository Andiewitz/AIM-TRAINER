FIELD GUIDE

Make every rep count.

Choose a drill and start. Your browser will lock the mouse to the range. Move to aim and click to shoot. In tracking, hold the mouse button on the moving target. In AWP crossing, right-click to scope and shoot the ball through the gap between the doors; your AWP cycles between shots.

Press Escape to pause. If mouse lock fails, click Resume and allow the browser prompt. Scores are saved in this browser on this computer.

This trainer isolates aim; it does not simulate CS2 weapon recoil, movement, hitboxes, or game physics. Match your in-game sensitivity as a starting point, then check the feel.
